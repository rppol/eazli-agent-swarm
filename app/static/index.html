What the labels mean
stated
The listing gave dimensions with explicit axis labels. Most trustworthy.
parsed
Dimensions read from an unlabelled field. The numbers are usually right; which one is the depth is a guess.
conflicted
The listing contradicts itself across two fields. Not eligible to be placed.
missing
No published dimensions. Never reported as fitting.
needs tipping
It reaches the room only carried on its side, or stood on end at the corridor turn.
tight fit
Under 3 cm to spare at some opening. Measure before ordering.
unverified
A rule could not be applied, so nothing is claimed. Not the same as a pass.

Nothing here is decided in the browser. Every move posts the arrangement to /plan/swap; the verdict comes back from app/geometry.py — the same code the tests and the agents use.
